One of you gets a steady paycheck. The other gets paid whenever clients pay. Most couples budgets assume two regular salaries, so the self-employed partner's tax money gets spent and every slow month turns into a row.

This Self-Employed Couples Budget fixes the order: the tax pot comes off first, the self-employed partner pays themself the same steady amount every month (a buffer covers the low months), then shared bills are split fairly from that steady pay. One number tells you what is safe to spend together.
