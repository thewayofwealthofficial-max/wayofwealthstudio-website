## Ways ours beats it
1. **Opens where you expect.** One plain .xlsx for Excel and Google Sheets, a README that says so, no Canva links anywhere (fixes the 1-star).
2. **Looks the same in Google Sheets.** Cells-only design: no shapes, data bars or icon sets; LibreOffice round-trip check showed 0 errors and 10/10 key cells identical, 5/5 charts kept (benchmark warns its formatting may differ).
3. **Profit per hour, not just margin.** Pricing Check adds your share of chair rent and bills to every service, flags anything under your target pay per hour and gives the exact price that would pay you (benchmark: break-even and recommended price only).
4. **Tips split by cash and card**, on the log, the Monthly Summary and the Dashboard (benchmark: no tip tracking shown).
5. **Supplies & Stock with reorder levels** for gel, acrylic, tips and disposables, a stock value, and a "reorder soon" list on the Dashboard (benchmark has no stock tab).
6. **Monthly Money Check-in**: the month written in plain words from your logs, spending vs plan by category, biggest overspend, goal progress and five reflection questions (benchmark: a separate PDF checklist).
7. **Tax set-aside that works in any country**: your own %, money actually moved vs suggested, "top up" warning (benchmark: none).
8. **Rent vs commission check** for chair and booth renters.
9. **Unlocked with no password**, formulas protected only against accidents (fixes the locked-tab complaint seen on a peer).
10. **Example + blank file**, a plain Start Here tab, and a qualified financial planner answering questions by email (benchmark: 1.0 customer-service score).
